# Research Notes Index

`NOTES.md` is an index, not a live roadmap or product manual.

The original research and design synthesis is preserved in scope as a dated
historical record:

- [Research synthesis recorded 2026-07-31](notes/2026-07-31-research-synthesis.md)

Use the current sources of truth instead:

| Question | Current record |
| --- | --- |
| What does the project do now? | [README.md](README.md) and [user documentation](docs/index.rst) |
| What work is maintained or being considered? | [PLANS.md](PLANS.md) |
| What changed in a release? | [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) |
| What claims are supported? | [CORRECTNESS.md](CORRECTNESS.md) |
| Why was a durable choice made? | [Architecture decisions](adr/README.md) |

New research reviews should be dated and stored as separate records. Do not
rewrite the 2026-07-31 synthesis to make it sound current.
