# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want participation in SynthPopCan to be safe, respectful, and useful for
people across software development, statistics, social science, and the
humanities. We welcome contributors with different levels of technical
experience and different disciplinary vocabularies.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Make room for questions and explain unfamiliar terms.
- Critique ideas, methods, and code without attacking people.
- Respect privacy, research ethics, data licences, and access restrictions.
- Accept correction, acknowledge impact, and help repair harm.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, sustained
disruption, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private
information without permission are not acceptable. Do not pressure anyone to
share restricted research materials or personal data.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, release
work, project communication, and public spaces where someone is representing
SynthPopCan.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to Darcy Quesnel at
`darcy.quesnel@gmail.com`. Do not open a public issue for a sensitive report.
Reports will be reviewed as promptly and impartially as practical, with the
reporter's privacy respected. Project maintainers may edit or remove content,
issue a warning, temporarily limit participation, or permanently exclude a
participant when needed to protect the community.

Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation. If the project maintainer is
the subject of a report, affected participants may also use GitHub's reporting
and support channels.
