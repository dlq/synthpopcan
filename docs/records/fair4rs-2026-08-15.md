# FAIR4RS Self-Assessment — 2026-08-15

**Assessment scope:** SynthPopCan `0.9.0` and the public repository state at
commit `80c32b18fdb3d49dc739ee2d045aaa33645503c7`\
**Assessment date:** 2026-08-15\
**Framework:** [FAIR Principles for Research Software v1.0](https://doi.org/10.15497/RDA00068)\
**Next routine review:** the `1.0.0` major release, or earlier after a material
stewardship change

This is a project self-assessment, not a FAIR certification. “Met” means that
public evidence addresses the principle for the assessed release. “Partial”
identifies a real gap; each gap is attached to a maintained implementation plan
or an explicit pre-1.0 decision.

## Priority Actions

1. Preserve and revalidate the completed archive-correction evidence whenever
   the prepared-model registry or publication tooling changes.
1. Capture the final `1.0.0` source and add its release DOI/SWHIDs after the
   release exists; do not invent pending identifiers.
1. Use real consumer evidence to decide whether another exchange standard or
   simulator adapter materially improves interoperability.
1. Reassess F1.1 and R3 when new publication objects or independent research
   workflows appear.
